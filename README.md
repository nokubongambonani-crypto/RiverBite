# RiverBite
ST10501378 - Angel Mbonani
## Project Overview
•	Brief history: River Bite opened in 2026 as a small riverside cafe. Over the years it grew into a neighbourhood bistro known for seasonal dishes and weekly live music.
### Website goals and objective
•	Website goals & objectives:
•	Main goal: Increase reservations and walk-in visits.
•	Specific goals:
1.	Increase monthly website visits by 30% in 6 months.
2.	Increase online reservations by 40% in 4 months.
3.	Grow newsletter signups to 1,000 in 6 months.
4.	4.	Make the site easy to use on phones.
#### Timeline and milestone
•	Week 1: Kick-off, content list, sitemap
•	Week 2: Low-fidelity wireframes, collect photos/menu.
•	Weeks 3–4: Design mock-ups & approval
•	Weeks 5–6: Development and CMS setup
•	Week 7: Testing (mobile, speed, forms)
•	Week 8: Launch and post-launch checks.
•	Milestones: M1 sitemap, M2 design approval, M3 staging site, M4 launch
